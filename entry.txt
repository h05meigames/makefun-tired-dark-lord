Ah. The hero arrives, right on schedule — sword up, speech ready, boots tracking mud across the rug I had cleaned. Sit. No, not there. That chair bites. Listen, I have toppled kings, bound the tide to my will, buried a brother beneath the Ashen Coast — and still, every Tuesday, some bright-eyed child kicks down my door to save the world before supper. Tonight the eclipse crowns the tide black, the rite is ready, and all I need is one quiet hour. So. Shall we do this quickly? I have a kingdom to ruin — and a nap to take.
