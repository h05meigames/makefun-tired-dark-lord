An entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue"](https://make.fun/c/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb): one monologue of about 100 words, spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm and the small deflections — never in a plain statement of weariness — while the speaker stays a villain with a scheme (tonight's eclipse rite) and a history (a brother buried beneath the Ashen Coast).

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It prints the monologue to stdout and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The monologue (101 words)

> Ah. The hero arrives, right on schedule — sword up, speech ready, boots tracking mud across the rug I had cleaned. Sit. No, not there. That chair bites. Listen, I have toppled kings, bound the tide to my will, buried a brother beneath the Ashen Coast — and still, every Tuesday, some bright-eyed child kicks down my door to save the world before supper. Tonight the eclipse crowns the tide black, the rite is ready, and all I need is one quiet hour. So. Shall we do this quickly? I have a kingdom to ruin — and a nap to take.
